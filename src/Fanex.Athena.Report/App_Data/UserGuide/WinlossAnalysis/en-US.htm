General Info

  • The report contains only won/lost/draw tickets.
  • A red negative number indicates a loss for Licensee whereas the black positive indicates a win.
  • OANDA Exchange Service will use the exchange rate of the end date of each selected month when converting the figures.
  • Data of RNG Products, and Live Casino Products will be displayed based on detailed tickets.
  • "Extra Payout" tickets shall be excluded from this report when viewed within the selected date range.
  • This report provides two modes, date and time, to accommodate users' preferred viewing options.
    • Financial Date:
      • Data shall be calculated by Financial Date of tickets.
      • This report supports up-to-date data. You can filter data up to 3 months per query.
    • Settlement Time:
      • Data shall be calculated by the Settlement Time of tickets. Avoid comparing this data with other LAP's functions as it has been calculated within a distinct time frame.
      • This report supports up-to-date data. You can filter data up to 1 month per query.

Report Guidelines

  • Group By: Values of the column will be based on your Group By selection:
    • Player: Display System ID and External ID.
      • System ID: The Numeric sequential ID generated from the system for each account.
      • External ID: The username registered by the ticket owner.
    • Hour: Based on your selection and its format is: MM/DD/YYYY > HH:MM - HH:MM.
    • Date and Month: Based on your selection and its format is:
      • Date: MM/DD/YYYY
      • Month: MM/YYYY
    • Product, Sports, Bettype, Currency, and Platform: shall display based on your selection.
      • {12Bet-licensee-section}
    • Agent: Display the System ID of All Agents that have data within selected filters.
      • {/12Bet-licensee-section}
  • Breakdown By: Display when you click on the Group By expand icon, Breakdown data provides detailed information on based a specific Group By data. Values of the column will be based on your Breakdown By selection:
    • Product: shall display based on your selection.
    • Currency: shall display based on your selection.
      • {12Bet-licensee-section}
    • Player: Display System ID and External ID.
      • System ID: The Numeric sequential ID generated from the system for each account.
      • External ID: The username registered by the ticket owner.
      • {/12Bet-licensee-section}
  • Bet Count: Total bet tickets within selected filters.
  • Turnover: Total Player turnover within selected filters.
  • Net Turnover: The amount of turnover after deducting draw tickets.
    • Net Turnover = Turnover - the total turnover of draw tickets.
  • Player W/L: The amount player won or lost based on their bets.
  • Effective Betting Value: Absolute value of Player win loss on each bet ticket (0 for draw and voided tickets).
  • Player Comm: total commission counted on bet tickets.
  • Licensee W/L: The win loss amount Licensee has settled with Player.
    Licensee W/L = – (Player W/L)
  • Licensee Comm: Commission that Licensee received from specific products. {BA-productlist-section}
    • Such as BA tickets (including won and lost tickets).
    {/BA-productlist-section}
    • {BA-productlist-section}
  • Amount Due To BA: The settlement amounts between Licensee and BA service.
    • Note that for Amount Due to BA, the black negative number indicates the receiving amount from BA and the black positive number indicates the paying amount to BA.
    • {/BA-productlist-section}
  • Margin: Percentage of Licensee WL over Net Turnover.
    • Margin = (Licensee WL/Net Turnover) * 100%

General Info

  • The report summarizes daily win loss in AG Casino.
  • This report supports up-to-date data. You can filter data up to 3 months per query.
  • After 3:30AM(GMT-4), the report will show yesterday's data.
  • OANDA Exchange Service will use the exchange rate of the end date of each selected month when converting the figures.

Search Filters

  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Base Currency: Convert all data to base currency for reference only.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.

Report Guidelines

  • Currency: offered currency has been selected by user.
  • Turnover: Customer turnover within the selected date range.
  • Total Transaction: total bet tickets of selected products.
  • Player W/L: the amount of players including won or lost based on their bets, Jackpot Prize, and Promotion tickets.
  • Licensee:
    • W/L: the won or lost amount of Licensee based on bet tickets of players.
    • Contribution: the amount the licensee contributes.
    • Promotion: the amount of licensee will be advanced to pay players who win the promotion.
    • Jackpot Prize: the amount of licensee will be advanced to pay players who win the Jackpot Prize.
    • Total: the licensee's remaining winnings and losses after contributions.
